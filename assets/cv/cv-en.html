Professional profile

Artificial intelligence specialist with over twenty years in technology and more than thirteen dedicated specifically to AI and machine learning (since 2012). Chief AI Officer at Teracloud, where I set the vision, architecture and AI adoption strategy for the company and its clients; alongside that role I maintain an independent practice in consulting, writing and public speaking on AI and data.

My work spans both technical construction —MLOps, data architectures, generative AI systems and agents in production— and the teaching and communication of those subjects to technical and non-technical audiences. Author of the D2M2 (Datera Data Maturity Model), a data maturity assessment framework used by Teracloud and other organizations, and of two books on AI leadership published in 2025, in Spanish and English.

My recent writing focuses on the public dimension of the problem: access to compute, to data and to AI education, and the re-skilling of junior professionals in the face of automation.

Areas of specialization

  • Generative AI and language models. Design and implementation of solutions on Amazon Bedrock, Anthropic and OpenAI; context engineering, agents, integration with APIs and knowledge bases.
  • MLOps and machine learning systems engineering. Infrastructure, pipelines and production deployment of machine and deep learning workloads.
  • Data architecture for AI. Multi-account data lakes, data warehouses and lakehouse architectures (S3, Redshift, Lake Formation, Apache Iceberg, Glue, DataZone).
  • Data maturity and governance. Author of the D2M2 model; diagnostic assessment and data modernization roadmaps as a prerequisite to any AI initiative.
  • AI risk, security and ethics. Shadow AI and corporate data exfiltration, responsible AI governance, assessment of organizational exposure.
  • AI, work and inequality. The impact of automation on the structure of employment; re-skilling junior professionals toward orchestration and oversight of AI systems; public access to compute and knowledge.
